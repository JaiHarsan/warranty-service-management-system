# Project Abstract

Consumer products typically come with warranties that guarantee repair or replacement within a specific timeframe. However, consumers often lose physical warranty documents, forget expiration dates, and overlook subtle hardware defects until after the warranty expires, leading to out-of-pocket expenses. 

**WarrantyCare** is a proactive Product Service Intelligence Platform that centralizes household product information. It offers a digital warranty wallet, rule-based product health checks, and service centre discovery. 

By running deterministic rule-based algorithms against user-submitted symptoms, WarrantyCare categorizes product health risks (GREEN, YELLOW, RED) and recommends timely interventions. It features prototype service-request generation connected to localized service centres. Developed using Vanilla JavaScript, Python (Django/DRF), and MongoDB, the system ensures strong user data isolation, PBKDF2 password security, and responsive cross-device usability.
